I specialize in procedures, forms, and reference material for operations, compliance, and product teams. My work starts with the people who will use the document. I interview subject matter experts, observe the task, and test drafts with real users before anything ships.

More recently I've brought a docs-as-code approach to my teams: writing in Markdown, reviewing changes in Git, and publishing automatically. This very site is built that way.

## What I bring

- **Plain language.** Clear, scannable writing that meets accessibility and plain-language standards.
- **Process thinking.** I map the workflow before I write a word, so the document matches how work actually happens.
- **Collaboration.** I'm comfortable leading reviews with engineers, managers, and legal and compliance teams.
