I'm a technical writer at Puget Sound Energy, where I support the Wildfire Risk Management program. I draft and refine operational procedures based on direction from subject matter experts, create the forms and templates that go with them, and maintain document control: numbering, versions, change requests, reviews, and approvals.

Before Puget Sound Energy, I wrote and edited specifications, standards, procedures, manuals, and training materials for engineering, hardware, and software teams at Bluetooth SIG, Parsons, Lithion Battery, and Arcata Associates.

I'm currently building my docs-as-code skills: writing in Markdown, tracking changes in Git, and publishing automatically. This portfolio is written in Markdown and published from GitHub, and keeping it up to date is part of how I'm learning.

## What I bring

- **Plain language.** Clear, scannable writing, checked against a style guide and a review checklist.
- **Process thinking.** I organize each document around the order work actually happens, with clear roles, decision points, and records.
- **Document control.** Templates, version control, change requests, and tracking that keep documents accurate and easy to find.
- **Collaboration.** I coordinate input and reviews with engineers, subject matter experts, program managers, and executive reviewers, and handle the writing, formatting, and coordination so experts can focus on the content.
