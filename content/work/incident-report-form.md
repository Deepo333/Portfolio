## Overview

A fillable PDF form that employees use to report workplace incidents and near misses. The form feeds directly into the safety team's review process.

## The problem

About a third of submitted reports were missing key details, so the safety team had to follow up by phone. The old form used internal jargon, asked for the same information twice, and put the most important questions at the end.

## My approach

- **Analyzed 50 past submissions** to see which fields were skipped or misunderstood.
- **Rewrote every label in plain language**, for example changing "Nature of occurrence" to "What happened?"
- **Reordered the form** to match how people remember an event: what happened, where and when, who was involved, then what was done.
- **Added help text and examples** under the fields people struggled with, and made essential fields required in the fillable version.

## The result

Incomplete submissions dropped sharply, follow-up calls became the exception, and the safety team now uses the form's structure for its digital version.
