## Overview

The PSE Wildfire Season Declaration Procedure (PSE-WRM-002) defines how Puget Sound Energy decides
when its Wildfire Season is active. It covers how conditions are monitored, who recommends and
approves a change in status, and what each group does once the status changes. It's part of the PSE
Wildfire Operating Plan. I drafted it for the Wildfire Risk Management (WRM) program, working from
subject matter expert direction.

## The problem

Each change in Wildfire Season status sets off mitigation and preparedness work across the company.
The decision draws on several weather and fire-danger indicators, and it involves wildfire liaison
managers, the meteorologist, operations leaders, and executive approval. Everyone in those roles needs
to know what triggers a change, who decides, and what they're responsible for doing next.

## My approach

- Organized the procedure into the standard controlled-document sections: purpose, scope, terms and
  acronyms, roles and responsibilities, prerequisites, procedure, records, references, and revision
  history.
- Put the seasonal indicators in a table with their activation and rescission thresholds, so readers
  can compare the criteria side by side.
- Wrote each stage of the decision as numbered steps: monitoring, recommendation, approval,
  modification, and implementation. A process overview figure shows how the stages connect.
- Assigned responsibilities by role title in a RACI matrix, and listed the implementation actions for
  each role group in a table.
- Defined every acronym and specialized term in tables, and described each wildfire intelligence source
  the decision relies on in the references section.
- Kept the document under document control, with a document number, revision, effective date, and
  revision history.
