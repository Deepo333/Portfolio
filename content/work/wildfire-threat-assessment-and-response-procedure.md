## Overview

The PSE Wildfire Threat Assessment and Response Procedure (PSE-WRM-003) sets out how Puget Sound Energy
monitors wildfires during Wildfire Season, judges the threat to its electric, generation,
telecommunications, and gas infrastructure, and responds. It also sets up a weekly Wildfire Situation
Briefing for leadership. I drafted it for the Wildfire Risk Management (WRM) program, working from
subject matter expert direction.

## My approach

I set out who monitors and when, including shift handoffs, and stated the trigger plainly: any fire
within about 2 miles of a PSE asset is evaluated. Three Potential Impact Levels (High, Moderate, and
Low) each have their criteria in a callout box, with a decision-precedence table for choosing between
them. Responses are cumulative, so each higher level adds to the actions of the level below. Contact
numbers are removed from this public copy.
