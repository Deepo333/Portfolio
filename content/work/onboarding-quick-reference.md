## Overview

A printable, two-page quick-reference guide that covers the systems, contacts, and tasks every new employee needs in their first month.

## The problem

New hires received a 40-page handbook on day one. Most didn't read it, and managers were answering the same handful of questions over and over: how to request access, who to call for IT help, and where to find the forms they needed.

## My approach

- **Collected real questions** from six months of onboarding emails and help-desk tickets, then ranked them by frequency.
- **Wrote task-based sections** ("Get access to systems," "Submit your timesheet") instead of organizing by department.
- **Designed for scanning**, using short headings, consistent icons, and a contacts table people can find at a glance.
- **Reviewed with HR and IT** to confirm every link and contact was correct.

## The result

The guide is now part of every new hire's welcome packet. Managers reported fewer repeat questions, and the HR team adapted the format for two other audiences.
