## Overview

Form PSE-WRM-F03 is how people request a revision, update, correction, or enhancement to a controlled
document in Puget Sound Energy's Wildfire Risk Management (WRM) program. It's part of the document
control process I maintain for the program.

## My approach

The form asks for the requester and the document first, then classifies the change with drop-down
lists for document type, reason, and priority. Each requested change gets its own block, located by
section, paragraph, and page, with clear rules: exact replacement wording for small revisions, a
summary for large ones, and a tracked-changes copy for every request. It ends with required
acknowledgments and signatures from the requester, their supervisor or manager, and the WRM Manager.
