## Overview

Form PSE-WRM-F03 is how people ask for a revision, update, correction, or enhancement to a controlled
document in Puget Sound Energy's Wildfire Risk Management (WRM) program. It's part of the document
control process I maintain for the program.

## The problem

A writer can only act on a change request that says which document, exactly where, what the new
wording should be, and whether the requester's manager agrees. A request missing any of that has to go
back for clarification, so the form asks for all of it up front.

## My approach

- Asked for the requester and the document first, then a change classification with drop-down lists
  for document type, reason for change, and priority.
- Gave each requested change its own block, located by section name, section number, paragraph or
  list number, and page, with room for up to six changes.
- Set clear rules for describing changes: exact replacement wording for small revisions, a high-level
  summary for large ones, and a tracked-changes copy of the document for every request.
- Ended with an attachment checklist, four required acknowledgments, and signatures from the
  requester, their supervisor or manager, and the WRM Manager.
- Marked required fields with an asterisk and put the submission instructions on the form itself.

## The result

Version 05/2026 is a fillable PDF with drop-down lists, check boxes, and signature fields.
