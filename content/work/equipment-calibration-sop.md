## Overview

A standard operating procedure (SOP) for calibrating bench equipment at the start of each shift. It replaced a set of handwritten notes and tribal knowledge with a single controlled document that technicians use at the workstation.

## The problem

Each shift calibrated equipment slightly differently. The existing instructions were a scanned, three-year-old document that skipped steps experienced staff did from memory. New technicians made frequent errors, and two audit findings traced back to inconsistent calibration records.

## My approach

- **Observed the task** on all three shifts and recorded where people hesitated or improvised.
- **Interviewed** two senior technicians and the quality manager to capture the reasons behind each step.
- **Structured the procedure** around the physical workflow, with one action per step, warnings placed before the steps they apply to, and a sign-off table that doubles as the calibration record.
- **Usability-tested** a draft with two new hires and revised the wording wherever they paused.

## The result

The SOP passed quality review on the first submission and became the template for six other procedures in the lab. In the following quarter, calibration-related errors dropped noticeably and the related audit findings were closed.
