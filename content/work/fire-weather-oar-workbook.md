## Overview

The Operational Action Recommendation (OAR) workbook is completed before each Operations Planning Call
under the [Critical fire weather operations procedure](/work/critical-fire-weather-operations-procedure/).
It brings the fire weather summary, the System Operations and Load Office recommendations, changes made
after the call, staffing, and per-asset risk and actions into one record, followed by the Level 2
Escalation Checklist.

## My approach

The asset table has one row per circuit, transmission line, or substation, with weather and risk
indicators completed by the meteorologist and fire behavior analyst, and protective actions completed
by System Operations and the Load Office. The escalation checklist is organized into six phases, with a
responsible role, notes, status, and completion fields for every step, and ends with a sign-off. The PDF
is fillable, with a sample row showing how to complete the asset table.
