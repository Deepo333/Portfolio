## Overview

A 30-minute training deck, with speaker notes, that introduced support staff to a new ticketing system. Team leads presented it in small groups during the two weeks before launch.

## The problem

The vendor's training materials covered every feature but didn't reflect how our teams actually worked. Staff were anxious about the switch, and leadership wanted consistent training without pulling everyone into a single long session.

## My approach

- **Identified the five tasks** each person would do on day one and built the deck around them.
- **Showed what changes and what doesn't** early on, to reduce anxiety about the switch.
- **Wrote detailed speaker notes** so any team lead could present the deck consistently.
- **Piloted the session** with one team and trimmed slides that raised more questions than they answered.

## The result

All teams were trained before launch. Help-desk tickets about the new system were lower than forecast in the first month, and the deck was reused for new-hire training.
