## Overview

This form records every Wildfire Season status change made under the
[Wildfire Season Declaration Procedure](/work/wildfire-season-declaration-procedure/). The wildfire
liaison managers and the meteorologist complete and sign it, and the Senior Vice President of Energy
Operations, or a delegate, approves it.

## My approach

I ordered its nine numbered sections to follow the decision, from the experts consulted and the
indicators considered through the action, region, mitigations, and signatures. The indicator checklist
matches the indicators table in the procedure, so both documents use the same terms. The approval
rules, including approval by a delegate and the up-to-seven-day implementation window, are printed on
the form itself.
