## Overview

Form PSE-WRM-F01 records every Wildfire Season status change made under the
[Wildfire Season Declaration Procedure](/work/wildfire-season-declaration-procedure/). The wildfire
liaison managers and the meteorologist fill it in and sign it, and the Senior Vice President of Energy
Operations, or a delegate, approves it.

## The problem

A season declaration draws on several experts, several seasonal indicators, and a written rationale,
and it puts mitigations in place across operations. All of that needs to be captured the same way
every time, in the same terms the procedure uses.

## My approach

- Ordered the nine numbered sections to follow the decision: who prepared it, which experts were
  consulted, which indicators were considered, the sources behind the recommendation, the action, the
  region, the mitigations, the recommenders' signatures, and approval.
- Matched the list of seasonal indicators to the indicators table in the procedure, and pointed to that
  table by name, so the form and the procedure use the same terms.
- Used check boxes for the experts, indicators, regions, and mitigations; a single choice for the
  action (activate, modify, or rescind); and signature fields for each recommender and the approver.
- Put the approval rules on the form itself: a delegate may approve, and full implementation of the
  mitigations can take up to seven days.
