## Overview

This field brief prepares account executives and solutions engineers for first and second calls with
counter-drone (C-UAS) integrators about Echodyne's EchoShield radar. I wrote it as an independent work
sample using only public sources: Echodyne's datasheet, product page, and newsroom, plus press coverage.
It is not an official Echodyne document.

## The problem

Datasheet specs don't sell on their own. Before a call, a seller needs to know who they're talking to,
what a number like a "10 Hz update rate" means to that customer, and how to answer common objections,
in something they can read in about four minutes.

## My approach

- Opened with a 30-second summary and the one idea to land: a counter-drone system is only as good as
  the track it starts with.
- Profiled the primary and secondary buyers: what each is trying to do and what they worry about.
- Translated each datasheet spec into plain customer language, with why it matters to the buyer.
- Added a 45-second talk track, six discovery questions with what to listen for, objection handling,
  proof points, and when the product may not be the right fit.
- Flagged the claims that need expert sign-off right in the brief, and listed each one in a writer's
  notes page as a question for a named owner in Sales leadership, Product, or Solutions Engineering.
- Proposed how to measure success, and listed every source.

## The result

The brief is ready for subject matter expert review. It's marked as a draft until the five open items
in the writer's notes are confirmed by the right teams.
