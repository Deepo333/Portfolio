## Overview

This field brief prepares account executives and solutions engineers for first and second calls with
counter-drone (C-UAS) integrators about Echodyne's EchoShield radar. I wrote it as an independent work
sample using only public sources. It is not an official Echodyne document.

## My approach

The brief opens with a 30-second summary and one idea to land: a counter-drone system is only as good
as the track it starts with. It profiles the buyers, translates each datasheet spec into plain
customer language, and adds a talk track, discovery questions, and objection handling. Claims that
need expert sign-off are flagged in the brief and listed on a writer's notes page, each with a named
owner.
