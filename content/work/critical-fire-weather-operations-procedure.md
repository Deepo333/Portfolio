## Overview

The Critical Fire Weather Operations Procedure sets out how Puget Sound Energy reduces the likelihood
of its electrical equipment starting a wildfire when fire weather conditions are high. It defines four
Operational Levels, from routine monitoring through a Public Safety Power Shutoff (PSPS), with the
actions, decision-makers, and approvals for each. I prepared Revision 03, which updated the Level 2
processes for enabling Enhanced Powerline Settings (EPS).

## My approach

A summary table maps each level's weather indicators to its recommended actions and approval path,
and a timeline figure gives a visual overview of the actions in the hours leading up to a possible
PSPS. The Level 2
escalation is backed by a step-by-step checklist that names a responsible role for every action, and
a RACI matrix, logging requirements, and a records table keep accountability clear.
