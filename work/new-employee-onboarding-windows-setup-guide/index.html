New employee onboarding: Windows setup guide

A 23-slide visual guide that walks new Bluetooth SIG employees through setting up their Windows laptop, from the first setup screen to installing apps from the Company Portal.

Overview

This slide deck welcomes new Bluetooth SIG employees and walks them through setting up their company Windows laptop on their own. It covers Windows setup, Microsoft Authenticator, Windows Hello, and the Company Portal, followed by troubleshooting steps and where to get help.

My approach

The guide is organized around what a new employee sees on screen, with one step per slide and a screenshot of each screen. It calls out the choices that matter most, such as which account to sign in with, and sets expectations for waits, such as enrollment taking up to 30 minutes. The IT help desk contact appears at each point where setup can go wrong.
