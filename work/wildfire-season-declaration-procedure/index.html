Overview

The PSE Wildfire Season Declaration Procedure defines how Puget Sound Energy decides when its Wildfire Season is active. It covers how conditions are monitored, who recommends and approves a change in status, and what each group does once the status changes. I drafted it for the Wildfire Risk Management (WRM) program, working from subject matter expert direction.

My approach

I organized it into the standard controlled-document sections, from purpose and scope through references and revision history. The seasonal indicators sit in one table with their activation and rescission thresholds, and each stage of the decision is written as numbered steps, with a process overview figure. Responsibilities are assigned by role title in a RACI matrix, and every acronym and specialized term is defined in a table.
