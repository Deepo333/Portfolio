  • Knowledge management

My processMy process

How I take a document from first input to a published, maintained version.

  1. 01

    Gather

    Collect information from subject matter experts, existing documentation, and the people who use it.

  2. 02

    Draft

    Structure the content for its audience, then write in clear, plain language.

  3. 03

    Review

    Route the draft to subject matter experts and stakeholders for technical review.

  4. 04

    Refine & approve

    Resolve comments, edit for quality and consistency, and secure final approval.

  5. 05

    Publish

    Publish the approved version to its designated repository or platform.

  6. 06

    Maintain

    Manage versions and change requests, and run scheduled reviews to keep content accurate.
